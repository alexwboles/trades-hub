How they connect

Quotely → MaterialList

Win the quote in Quotely and turn it into a takeoff in MaterialList — line items become quantities and costs, so nothing gets forgotten at the supply house.

MaterialList → PermitPilot

With the job specced and stocked, check PermitPilot before work starts — know exactly which permits you need and get the applications in early.

PermitPilot → SafetyCheck

Permits approved means work begins. SafetyCheck has the trade's daily checklist, the PPE walk-through, and the toolbox talk ready for the crew.

SafetyCheck → FleetLog

A safe crew needs safe trucks. FleetLog keeps the rigs serviced and road-ready — overdue maintenance flags before it strands someone on the highway.

FleetLog → SiteVisit

Roll up to the site and document it in SiteVisit — photos, notes, and follow-ups captured while the details are fresh.

SiteVisit → Quotely

The visit's quote draft feeds straight back into Quotely — and the next job runs through the whole stack again.
